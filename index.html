Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What are your rates like?

Rate is 100% deal- and borrower-dependent — a strong borrower with a thin deal won't get top terms, and a great deal with a weak borrower won't either. We don't quote a "teaser" rate that only applies to platinum-tier borrowers, because it misleads anyone who won't actually qualify. Real numbers come from a term sheet built for your specific deal.

What's the minimum deal size?

$75K up to $15M+, across residential and commercial. We're generally not the right fit under $100K or with credit below 660.

What is Middle Market lending, and when do you use it?

It's not our starting point or our only offering — it's the tool we reach for when a deal doesn't fit the traditional bank box: low cash flow/DSCR, projection-based, and asset-based deals among them. It closes faster and costs meaningfully less than hard money, with rate buy-down options available.

Do you do gap lending, second-position lending, or investment HELOCs?

No to all three. We're a primary-debt lender — not a gap/second-position or equity-partnership structure.

How long does approval take?

Term sheet within 1 business day for a standard request; up to 1 week for commercial deals.
