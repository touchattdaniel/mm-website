Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

This policy explains what Maple and Millwood Consultants ("M&M," "we," "us") collects when you submit a deal inquiry through our website or an online ad, how we use it, and what rights you have.

Who We Are

Maple and Millwood Consultants is a capital advisory and loan brokerage serving real estate investors, with a focus on Florida and Kansas markets. We are a broker — we match your deal with third-party capital partners; we do not fund or service loans directly.
Contact: Daniel Touchatt, daniel@mapleandmillwood.com, (316) 680-7805.

What We Collect

When you submit a form on mapleandmillwood.com, respond to one of our ads, or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Contact information — name, phone number, email address
  • Property and deal details — property address, type, use, and estimated value
  • Financing details — loan type, requested amount, purchase price, rehab budget, exit strategy, and related deal terms
  • Borrower and financial background — investor experience, entity type, approximate credit-score range, liquid funds, and similar underwriting information you choose to share
  • Documents you upload — such as a purchase contract, if you attach one to your submission
  • How you found us — such as the ad or campaign that brought you to our site (tracked via URL parameters, not third-party data purchases)

We do not pull a hard credit report, and we do not request Social Security numbers or banking credentials, through this form. Any such information is collected later, directly, and only with your explicit authorization as part of underwriting.

How We Use It

We use the information you provide to (1) evaluate your financing request, (2) match your deal to potential capital partners suited to it, (3) communicate with you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry, and (4) keep records required by applicable lending and consumer-protection regulations.

Who We Share It With

We share the minimum information necessary with capital partners we believe may be a fit for your deal. We do not sell your information to data brokers or unrelated marketers. We use a small number of service providers to operate our intake and scheduling process — including form/workflow automation (n8n), record-keeping (Airtable), and email (Google) — under standard confidentiality terms. If you use our ads on Meta platforms (Facebook/Instagram), Meta may also process limited technical data (such as ad interaction events) under its own privacy policy, separate from this one.

Cookies & tracking: Our site may use standard analytics and ad-measurement tools (such as the Meta Pixel) to understand which ads led to a visit or submission. These tools do not give us access to your Facebook or Instagram account content — only aggregate performance data about ad campaigns.

Text, Call & Email Consent

By submitting a form, you consent to be contacted by phone, text message, and email about your financing inquiry, at the number and email address you provided. Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of receiving any loan or service.

How Long We Keep It

We retain lead and deal information for up to 36 months after your most recent interaction with us, after which we delete or anonymize records not otherwise required to be kept by regulation.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing daniel@mapleandmillwood.com. Florida and Kansas residents have rights under applicable state consumer-protection law. Residents of California, Colorado, Virginia, and other states with comprehensive privacy statutes (CCPA, CPA, VCDPA, and similar) have rights under those laws as applicable.

Do-Not-Contact Requests

You may ask to be added to our internal do-not-contact list at any time by replying STOP to a text or emailing daniel@mapleandmillwood.com.

Business-Purpose Lending Notice

All financing arranged through Maple and Millwood Consultants is business-purpose lending to entity borrowers (LLC, LP, or corporation). We do not arrange consumer mortgages or financing for owner-occupied residential purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available at this URL, with the effective date shown above.
